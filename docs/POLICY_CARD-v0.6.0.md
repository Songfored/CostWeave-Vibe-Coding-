# CostWeave v0.6.0 policy card

The v0.6.0 tournament is evaluation-only. It does not promote a policy, change the stable runtime route, call a provider, or make a commercial claim. Claims require a test partition, at least E1 evidence, an adequate independent sample, complete cost/latency fields, blind-field protection, and a paired statistical gate.

The bundled E0 seed must remain `insufficient_sample`/not claimable.
